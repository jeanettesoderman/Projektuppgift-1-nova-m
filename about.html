The story and creative approach behind Nova M Music Studio

About Nova M Music Studio

Nova M Music Studio creates original music for video, digital content and visual storytelling. The focus is on creating music that supports the mood, story and identity of each project, rather than using generic background music that may not fully match the content.

Behind Nova M Music Studio is Jeanette Söderman, a creator with a strong interest in music, visual storytelling and digital production. Each project begins with an idea, a mood or a story and develops into music that is created to fit that specific project.

Creative Approach

Each project begins with the story, mood and purpose behind the content. From there, the music is shaped to complement the visuals and create a coherent experience.
